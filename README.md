# Hey, I'm Clowd.



I'm a student, Linux enthusiast, and curious tinkerer who enjoys exploring how technology works and finding ways to make it better.

I believe good engineering isn't just about making something work. It's about making it **efficient, thoughtful, and beautiful**.

### `01` — What I do

- **Linux & open source** — Exploring the Linux ecosystem and making my computing environment my own.
- **Desktop customization** — Bringing functionality and visual elegance together through thoughtful design.
- **Lightweight software** — Exploring ways to make software feel fast, efficient, and practical.
- **Experimentation** — Learning by building, breaking, troubleshooting, and trying again.

### `02` — What I value

```text
Simplicity over clutter.
Efficiency over waste.
Privacy over surveillance.
Understanding over imitation.
Elegance without compromise.
```

### `03` — Currently exploring

- Linux desktop development and Wayland
- Lightweight tools and custom workflows
- Open-source software and system optimization
- Writing better code and learning through projects

### `04` — My philosophy

I like the contrast between engineering and aesthetics: the complexity beneath the surface and the simplicity presented to the user.

The best solutions don't force us to choose between the two.

```text
        LOGIC  +  CREATIVITY
                |
                v
       FUNCTIONALITY × DESIGN
                |
                v
           BETTER THINGS
```

### `05` — Around here

You'll find experiments, configurations, personal projects, and things I'm learning along the way.

Not everything needs to be perfect to be worth building. Every project is an opportunity to understand something a little better.
